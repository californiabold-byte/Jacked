NEON PHYSIQUE TRACKER — ANDROID INSTALL GUIDE

This is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA), not a signed native APK.
It works on Android through Chrome and stores your tracking data locally in that browser on that device.

TO INSTALL:
1. Upload the contents of this folder to an HTTPS static host (for example GitHub Pages or Netlify).
2. Open the hosted HTTPS address in Chrome on your Android phone.
3. Open Chrome's three-dot menu and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
4. Launch NEON Physique from your home screen.

The app includes:
- Dashboard and daily meal check-ins
- Editable calorie and macro targets
- Workday meal schedule and water tracker
- Four-day upper/lower workout plan with weight logging
- Progress log for weight, waist, sleep and notes
- CSV export of progress entries
- Seven-day grocery checklist
- Offline caching after the first successful load

IMPORTANT:
- Data is saved in local browser storage. Clearing browser data or changing phones may remove it; use CSV export for progress backup.
- Nutrition values are estimates, not label-verified totals. Check your product labels and adjust targets based on your results.
- The app is not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice.
