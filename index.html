Progress rules

Weigh each morning after the bathroom and compare weekly averages. A reasonable initial fat-loss pace is about 0.5–1 lb/week. If weight and waist are unchanged for 2–3 weeks, consider a small calorie adjustment. If strength and recovery collapse, reassess the deficit.
